## Brand & Style

This design system powers an interactive student learning and classroom analytics portal. The visual language blends academic rigor with motivational gamification, striking an intentional balance between institutional trust and energetic, self-directed exploration.

Drawing from modern EdTech leaders, the aesthetic merges structured clarity with tactile, celebratory feedback loops. Interfaces feel bright, responsive, and approachable for K–12 and higher-ed cohorts without appearing juvenile. Precision analytics for instructors sit harmoniously alongside progress rings, streak badges, and milestone celebrations for students.

The primary style combines **Modern Tactile Minimalism** with subtle **Frosted Depth**:
- Crisp, hyper-clean surface tiers that maximize focus and minimize cognitive friction.
- Tactile, pressable micro-interactions that make learning progression feel tangible.
- Strategic flashes of saturated energy (amber streaks, mastery emeralds) to signal achievement against a dependable royal blue foundation.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate sense of competence and vibrancy. The core structure relies on high-contrast, clean slate canvases accented by rich chromatic bursts.

- **Primary (`#0062E3`)**: A calibrated Royal Blue representing academic clarity, authority, and confidence. Used for focal call-to-actions, active navigation highlights, and core brand milestones.
- **Secondary (`#5B46F6`)**: A vivid Indigo/Violet that adds an intellectual, creative energy. Deployed for progress indicators, interactive exercise widgets, and student-facing exploration modes.
- **Tertiary (`#059669`)**: A grounded Emerald reserved strictly for mastery states, completed objectives, passing performance badges, and positive momentum.
- **Neutral (`#121826`)**: A deep slate black that provides crisp readability across headers and body copy, avoiding the starkness of pure black while guaranteeing WCAG AAA contrast ratios against light surfaces.

### Functional Roles & Named Accents
- `streak-amber` (`#F59E0B`) and `streak-glow` (`#FEF3C7`) communicate daily practice, live session momentum, and active achievements.
- `surface-canvas` (`#F8FAFC`) keeps visual noise low across complex data views, with pure `#FFFFFF` cards lifting key modules into focus.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy couples **Plus Jakarta Sans** for display, headlines, and compact UI labels with **Inter** for sustained instructional reading and dense analytics dashboards.

- **Plus Jakarta Sans**: Infuses warmth, friendly curvature, and contemporary energy into titles and metrics. Its geometric structure feels encouraging yet disciplined.
- **Inter**: Acts as the neutral, high-legibility engine for problem sets, reading comprehension passages, analytics tooltips, and tabular reporting.
- Numbers within leaderboards, analytics meters, and timers should enable tabular lining figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to prevent jittering during active updates.

## Layout & Spacing

The system runs on an 8-point structural cadence nested inside a 12-column responsive fluid grid for desktop and tablet, collapsing to a 4-column layout on mobile devices.

- **Desktop (1024px and up)**: 12-column grid with `1.5rem` gutters and a maximum content container width of `1280px`. Left rail remains persistent for classroom roster/navigation.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px)**: 8-column grid with `1.25rem` gutters and `1.5rem` margins. Navigation consolidates into a responsive icon-rail or top bar.
- **Mobile (below 768px)**: 4-column flow with `1rem` gutters and `1rem` outer canvas padding. Action buttons, interactive answer selections, and progression bars snap to full-width modules.

Interior card paddings use `space-md` (`1rem`) on compact views and scale to `space-lg` (`1.5rem`) on classroom analytics cards to preserve breathing room.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through clean tactile stacking rather than heavy darkness. Elements evoke an intuitive, touch-friendly physical quality that invites engagement.

- **Level 0 (Canvas Base)**: Uses `surface-canvas` (`#F8FAFC`). Flat and restful.
- **Level 1 (Cards & Modules)**: Pure white background with a crisp perimeter line (`1px solid #E2E8F0`) and an ambient, low-opacity drop shadow: `0 2px 4px -1px rgba(18, 24, 38, 0.04), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(18, 24, 38, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Elements & Floating Panels)**: Raised buttons and dropdown overlays utilize a tactile base: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(0, 98, 227, 0.08), 0 10px 15px -3px rgba(18, 24, 38, 0.08)`.
- **Tactile Button Press Effect**: Primary actions feature a subtle 2px solid bottom offset in a slightly deeper shade. On active/pressed states, the element translates down `1px` to provide immediate physical feedback.
- **Mastery Glow**: Cards achieving 100% completion or active streaks emit an ambient, diffuse halo using `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.15)` or `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.15)`.

## Shapes

The design uses a balanced `roundedness: 2` scale, pairing approachable rounded rectangles for structures with fully pill-shaped interactive tokens for status and micro-actions.

- **Standard Cards and Modals**: Styled with `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / 16px) corners to keep large containers friendly and modern.
- **Buttons, Progress Pills, and Badges**: Transition to full pill shapes (`rounded-full` / 9999px) to convey tactile affordance, playful reward mechanisms, and rapid tappability.
- **Input Fields & Data Cells**: Styled with standard `0.5rem` (8px) corners to retain formal alignment with tabular data columns.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Pill-shaped or rounded-lg with `primary_color_hex` (`#0062E3`), white text, bold label styling, and a 2px simulated 3D edge (`#004BB0`). Hover deepens background slightly; active press shifts the element downwards by 1px.
- **Secondary Action**: Bordered button with `1.5px solid #0062E3`, transparent background, and `#0062E3` text. On hover, fills with `surface-tint` (`#EFF6FF`).
- **Streak & Reward Button**: Pill button filled with `streak-amber` (`#F59E0B`), featuring a flame or star icon, dark text, and an energetic, tactile state.

### Badges & Pill Chips
- **Streak Tracker**: Amber-tinted pill (`#FEF3C7` background, `#B45309` text) with a persistent icon prefix.
- **Mastery Status**: Emerald pill (`#D1FAE5` background, `#065F46` text) signaling verified skill completion.
- **Classroom Filter Chips**: Neutral border with subtle hover lift. When selected, transitions to `primary_color_hex` with white text and smooth spring animations.

### Cards & Content Modules
- Built on `#FFFFFF` with a 1px border (`#E2E8F0`).
- Feature clear header divisions, generous `1.5rem` internal padding, and distinct bottom utility rows for action prompts or mastery progress bars.
- Interactive learning cards elevate smoothly by -2px on hover with an ambient primary tint.

### Inputs & Form Elements
- **Text Inputs**: Height 44px (mobile-friendly), clean `#FFFFFF` fill, 1.5px border in `#CBD5E1`, with a distinct focus ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(0, 98, 227, 0.2)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: 20px controls with a 2px stroke. Active state fills with `#0062E3` displaying a crisp white checkmark. Spring animated transition.

### Progress & Mastery Trackers
- Segmented progress bars with rounded terminal ends. Unfilled track uses `#E2E8F0`, while active fill displays a vibrant gradient from `#0062E3` to `#5B46F6`.
- Milestone knots along the bar ignite with `mastery-emerald` once completed.
